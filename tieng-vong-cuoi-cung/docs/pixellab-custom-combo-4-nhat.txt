Chung (đầu mỗi mô tả): Facing right, pure side view. Keep the character EXACTLY as the reference (white mask, white ponytail, dark scarf, charcoal tunic, wrapped forearms and boots); only add one straight short silver sword in each hand. Do not add, remove or change any other detail. Near arm = character's LEFT arm, drawn in front of the body; far arm = RIGHT arm, behind the body. Body animation only: no effects, no trails, no particles, no glow.

G1 – RIGHT (far) hand horizontal slash:
F1 ready stance, knees bent, both swords low. F2 wind-up: hips and shoulders twist back, the far right arm pulls its sword back at shoulder height, blade horizontal pointing left; the near left arm stays tucked in front of the belly, its sword angled down. F3 hips snap forward, far arm sweeps the sword horizontally from behind toward the front at chest height. F4 impact: far arm fully extended to the right, blade horizontal, front foot planted. Near arm keeps guarding, never swings. F5-F6 far sword lowers to the front hip, settle back to ready stance.

G2 – LEFT (near) hand rising diagonal slash:
F1 ready stance. F2 wind-up: hips drop and twist back, the near left arm pulls its sword down-back beside the left thigh, tip pointing left-down; the far right arm stays tucked behind the waist, sword low. F3 front leg pushes, the near arm sweeps the sword past the front knee rising up-right. F4 impact: near arm fully extended high in front, sword tip up-right above head height, chest open. Far arm does not attack, sword low behind the hip. F5 near sword starts lowering to the chest; far arm lifts its sword up behind the shoulder. F6 hold.

G3 – RIGHT (far) hand overhead diagonal down slash:
F1 continuing from the previous pose, far right sword raised behind the shoulder. F2 wind-up: hips twist back, the far arm lifts the sword HIGH behind the head, elbow bent, tip pointing up-left; the near left arm keeps its sword low in front of the body. F3 hips snap forward, far arm chops over the shoulder down toward the front-right at 45 degrees, weight drops forward. F4 impact: far sword passes in front of the body, tip down-right near the front knee, front knee deeply bent. Near arm guards the belly. F5-F6 far sword drawn back low, recover.

G4 – BOTH hands scissor cut (finisher):
F1 OPEN SCISSORS: front knee deeply bent, body leaning forward, both hands near the front of the chest; both sword tips point forward (right): near sword angled up-right 45 degrees, far sword angled down-right 45 degrees, a wide sideways V like open scissor blades seen from the side; blades do NOT cross. F2 coil: elbows pulled close to the body, blades still open, head down, knees compressed. F3 closing: front foot stamps, upper blade chops down-right, lower blade lifts up-right, tips converge toward a point in front of the face. F4 CROSS: the blades slice past each other in front of the face forming a tall narrow X, near blade over far blade, lunging. F5 follow-through: arms continue past each other, blades separate at two heights, body low. F6 settle to ready stance.
