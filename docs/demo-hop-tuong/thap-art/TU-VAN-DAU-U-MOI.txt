TURRET MERGE — 85-UNIT TURRET HEAD ART DIRECTION BIBLE
Version 1.0 | English | Source: supplied 1500 x 864 pilot/head roster image

PURPOSE
Rebuild the turret heads so a player can identify rarity from the turret itself, identify its pilot through a secondary visual signature, and identify its gameplay function through a distinct top-down silhouette. The design is for 48 px in-game readability, round board footprint, right-pointing weapon axis, thick dark outlines, and chunky stylized sci-fi metal. Rarity follows the supplied roster, not any incidental colors already present in the current heads. The supplied image is a compact contact sheet: pilot color/motif notes below are visual interpretations at contact-sheet resolution, not claims about exact costume hex codes.

Percentages are target screen-visible color areas (exclude transparent background, black outlines and dark recesses), with ±3 percentage points tolerance. Glow refers to bright painted accents INSIDE the sprite, not broad bloom halos. Pilot accent 8% is a hard upper limit, not a quota. In a neutral or low-saturation pilot, use only 3–5%. Avoid an all-red common, all-purple rare, or all-gold mythic.

VALUE AND HIERARCHY
  1. All rarities use the same light steel base. Rarity hue appears at recurring standardized sites: outer rim, barrel collar, raised fins, connector ribs, small gem sockets.
  2. Preserve light/dark separation: steel highlights brighter than colored trims; recesses dark; only 1–2 glow points at COMMON/RARE.
  3. Put the rarity accent on the exterior perimeter and front muzzle where it survives downsampling. Keep the pilot accent INBOARD (core, grip, rear cartridge, tiny emblem, internal fluid, small optics).
  4. At a glance, the eye must first see WHITE / BLUE / PURPLE / GOLD / RED, then the pilot signature, then the weapon type. If the pilot color conflicts with rarity, reduce pilot area and saturation, never recolor the rarity ring.
  5. Do not use magenta #FF00FF anywhere on the turret: it is the exact background/key color.

ORNAMENT / SILHOUETTE RULES
  COMMON: 1 main body + 1 functional nozzle/skill shape; 0 decorative wings; 0–2 rivets; one simple collar; no colored rarity gem. Rounded compact outline; no ornate fins. Common can have a small skill-colored pilot indicator.
  RARE: 1–2 added functional panels; 2 blue perimeter strips or brackets; at most 1 small blue gem and 2 luminous ticks. Keep body simple and recognizable.
  EPIC: 2–3 raised modules; segmented purple outer ring; 2–4 purposeful fins/teeth; at most 2 purple gems and 3 light nodes. Moderate silhouette layering.
  LEGENDARY: 3–4 layered components; gold crown/crossbar/ray motif chosen for the SKILL, not copied across every turret; 4–6 silhouette details; 1 focal gold gem + 2–4 tiny lights. Gold is metallic edging, not yellow paint over the whole body.
  MYTHIC: 4–6 nested components; asymmetric or radial signature extensions; 6–9 bold silhouette features; 1 crimson focal seal + up to 5 tiny crimson nodes. Ornate but never visually noisy. Red armor remains trim/plate accents, not the entire shell.

GLOW / GEM RULES
  Rarity glow color is fixed by tier. Skill energy can have a different hue ONLY as a tiny internal core/beam/chemical (counted as pilot/skill accent). The largest luminous rim and perimeter indicator MUST use rarity glow. COMMON uses near-white light, not red or blue rarity lights. RARE has blue lights; EPIC purple; LEGENDARY warm gold; MYTHIC crimson. No exterior bloom, no blurry gradients, no detached VFX, no large aura. Glow is hard-edged cel shading at 48 px.

PILOT BLENDING ALGORITHM
  Choose ONE pilot anchor color and ONE signature motif from the contact sheet. Apply the anchor to an internal 3–8% area, such as a fluid reservoir, optic, fabric-wrap strip, or pilot badge. Translate the motif into functional metal geometry (goggles -> lens ring; clock -> indexed dial; hair spikes -> fins; wings -> side vanes; serpent -> coiled nozzle). Pilot motifs must never add random nonfunctional decorations. If the pilot's main color equals a DIFFERENT rarity tier (e.g. a red-haired COMMON), constrain it to one internal detail; if it equals the current rarity, still preserve the neutral body. Use colored skill fluid in an enclosed cavity, not on the outer frame. Prefer shape recognition over hue when conflicts arise.

48 PX PRODUCTION TEST
  Master draw at 192 x 192 (4x target), reduce to 48 x 48 using nearest-neighbor for silhouette checking, then inspect at 100% and 200%. Functional right-facing barrel axis is mandatory for weapons; support/auras/portals use a short right-pointing output port or a right-oriented opening rather than inventing a gun. Main silhouette must fit within a circular 40–44 px footprint, with at most 2 px deliberate projection for long guns. Minimum critical feature 2 px at game scale; no 1 px-only identity cues. Limit to 3 value groups per material and use a 2–3 px dark outline at 48 px where possible. Use a fixed light from upper-left. Rarity passes only if the turret is correctly classified without its card border or pilot portrait. Pilot passes only if a viewer can match a turret to its portrait among similar skills. Skill passes only if silhouettes remain distinguishable in grayscale.

ART REVIEW GATES
  Gate 1: rarity recognition (5/5 categories at 48 px in shuffled blind test).
  Gate 2: pilot matching (at least 80% correct within each tier, without names).
  Gate 3: skill recognition (at least 80% correct among similar weapon classes).
  Gate 4: production alignment (center, 2x2 grid consistency, right-facing axis, flat exact magenta key, no text).
  Gate 5: silhouette separation (no near-identical silhouette for two heads in same rarity).

The white body reads COMMON instantly, while the red-haired pilot still has a recognizable cue. No red external trim.

2) TELEPORTER (RARE): CURRENT -> Saturated purple-blue portal rings are attractive but visually compete with EPIC purple classification. AFTER -> Keep two distinct portal hoops as the skill signature, give both hoops broad silver bodies and standardized BLUE perimeter brackets, then place violet only INSIDE the portals. The player sees RARE blue first, the pilot's purple-blue portal identity second, and swap/teleport mechanics third.

3) BLACK HOLE (MYTHIC): CURRENT -> A purple vortex-centric head looks close to an EPIC purple turret despite being MYTHIC. AFTER -> Build a layered silver gravity well with six aggressive orbital blades and unmistakable CRIMSON outer seal plates/rune nodes; confine violet to the tiny event horizon. Keep the deep circular implosion silhouette, add mythic-tier construction density, and avoid flooding the body red.
