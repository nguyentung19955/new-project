Important distinction: A support does not necessarily buff adjacent tiles. Mana generators may produce resources globally, a cleanser may activate periodically, and merge units may act only on merging. Their artwork should express their actual behavior, not falsely promise an adjacent aura.

How to show buffs to four neighbors

Suggested selected-unit state

Use three distinct feedback states:

Normal gameplay: No continuous full-size aura and no permanent buff links. At most a small buff-status icon on an affected turret.

Selected/held support: Highlight the four orthogonal cells with a subtle teal tint and thin connection markers. Do not highlight diagonals unless the skill actually affects them.

Buff activation: Brief pulse traveling toward affected turrets for roughly 120–200 ms. No permanently animated laser lines.

Suggested code parameters: cell highlight alpha 0.12–0.18, link alpha 0.25–0.35, 1–1.5 px lines, only when selected or during activation.

For a 5×3 grid, compute row ± 1 or column ± 1 with bounds checks. A corner support affects up to two orthogonal cells, an edge support up to three, and an interior support up to four.

These rules prioritize truthful range communication over decorative glow.
