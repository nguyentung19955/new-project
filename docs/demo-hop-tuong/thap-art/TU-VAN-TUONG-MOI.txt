All values below are ORIGINAL DESIGN PROPOSALS, NOT VERIFIED STATS OF DCDW/RANDOM DICE/RUSH ROYALE.

EXECUTIVE DIRECTION
Do not add twenty bigger guns. Give the roster new decisions: geometry, target marking, risky economy, timed charge, enemy routing, and selective star manipulation. Preserve the neutral gunmetal shared base, one accent per head, white-to-red detail hierarchy, and the clear 48–55 px top-down silhouette. In a five-unit deck, each new unit must justify its slot through a distinct deck archetype.

COMMON BALANCE CONTRACT — READ FIRST
S = merge star (1..7). L = in-battle power-up level (1..5; assumed; if your game uses another level count, remap linearly). M = 1 + 0.10*(L-1). Every formula uses the CURRENT turret's S/L unless stated otherwise; percentages are percentage points when marked pp. Round displayed values to nearest integer, internal values may be fractional. Attack damage is given in baseline damage units (DU), calibrated against Cannon's level-1 star-1 hit = 100 DU; these are relative design values, not literal final damage values. In a typical normal attacker use 1 + 0.65*(S-1) shots-per-volley scaling or your standard star coefficient, but do NOT apply that coefficient again to explicit star-scaled skill damage formulas below. Normalized S=1,3,5,7 and L=1,5 examples are included where decisive.
- No ability grants stars beyond 7. Star grants cannot cause a merge trigger, including chain-triggering on-merge or on-summon abilities, unless a skill explicitly says otherwise. No self-copy, self-refund or recursive summon loops. One extra summon/merge/star event per original action; procs generated by another proc are tagged SECONDARY and cannot retrigger economic effects.
- At most 1 hard negative board status per enemy turret at a time (highest severity or newest; no stun chaining). Opponent board targeting is telegraphed 650 ms, non-lethal, avoids already affected slots and has a per-target immunity period of 8 s after expiration. No star-destruction on rivals in this proposal. PvP harassment uptime goal <=15% of a targeted unit; hard disable <=2.0 s.
- Attack speed positive cap +100%; damage bonus combined cap +150%; crit chance cap 65%; slow combined cap 45% (bosses 20%); vulnerability increase cap +30%. Instant death forbidden on bosses, elites and player turrets. Percent-current-HP damage boss cap 0.8% max HP per activation, elite cap 1.5% max HP.
- Buffs of the same named family: strongest applies; distinct families stack multiplicatively then respect cap. Support turrets affect exactly the four orthogonal neighbors only, unless the skill expressly modifies another topology. A turret may only receive one copy of a unique global aura. Summon starting cost 10 +10 per summon makes economy sensitive: simulate both 1/3/5-minute horizons and reroll frequency.
- PvP opponent manipulation must not silently steal stars or directly destroy a key legendary. In CO-OP two entrances merge into one enemy route: buffs are per-owner unless specifically said cross-player, and cross-player buffs/acts are visibly distinguished. SOLO uses PvE-compatible behavior and same caps.
- Event order: merge inputs consumed -> primary result created -> direct merge skill -> star grants -> secondary effects -> visual feedback. On transformations, apply a stable unit instance ID and clear target-ownership references before redistributing them. Every transform is logged for replay and server authority.
- Rarity art: PURPLE 1 signature attachment + modest trim; GOLD 1 major landmark plus 1 secondary ornament; RED distinctive dynamic silhouette and rare awaken animation. Shared 64-unit base is always neutral. No colored full-ring bases, no giant particle halos at idle. Accent area approximately <=12% purple, <=17% gold, <=20% red on head. All heads pivot at cell center.
- Three stage art on meaningful growth units: A dormant, B powered, C apex; stage assignment is triggered by stated mechanics (not merely rarity), and stage change animation 420–650 ms. For others use no stage art, only transient effects. 7-star should have a tiny universal star badge, not require distinct art for all 84.

EXISTING GOLD/RED AUDIT — REBALANCE BEFORE ADDING POWER CREEP
NOTE: Existing unit names and their role themes were supplied, not complete ability data. These are CONDITIONAL design risks; no claim that their live implementation is weak without telemetry.
GOLD
* Purifier: risks duplicating Cleanser. Make it offensive status conversion: each removed negative effect grants +6% damage for 5 s (up to 3 stacks), with 8 s per-unit cooldown; Cleanser remains immediate reactive cleanse.
* Sync Cannon: if just another DPS cannon, make its DPS depend on allied fire synchronization: 3 hits from distinct turret types within 1.5 s charge its next beam +35%, 2.0 s internal cooldown.
* Railgun: distinguish from Sniper/Siege Prism: long wind-up, one highest-HP elite pierce, armor-ignore 20%, tradeoff poor swarms. Do not let Piercing Prism outclass it on boss.
* Merge King and Twin Maker: potential duplicate extra-star/extra-summon economy. Merge King modifies merge rewards; Twin Maker generates a temporary linked twin with 50% damage, expires in 8 s and NEVER merges for profit.
* Star Breaker vs Star Cannon: assign Star Breaker anti-elite/shield break, Star Cannon sustained star-count multipliers. Separate targeting icons.
* Copycat vs Rainbow vs Evolver: risk too many flexible random/transform jokers. Copycat exact copy with identity tradeoff; Rainbow deck diversity payoff; Evolver timed 3-stage self-growth, never reroll utility.
* Werewolf: if generic burst, build rage meter from kills nearby, decay out of combat; visually shifting claw stages.
* Wall Projector: keep lane mitigation/support rather than another shield clone; short geometric barrier that blocks enemy projectiles only if those exist, otherwise armor-to-damage conversion for neighbors.
* Orbital: reserve for telegraphed path-wide strike with long cooldown and capped boss damage; not a second railgun.
RED
* Black Hole: owns displacement + grouping, NOT top percent-HP damage. Strong against swarms, boss displacement immunity; aim to give it visible red-tier spectacle without full-board obscuration.
* Overlord: make 3-stage long-charge sustained thunder DPS: A sealed, B charging, C ionized. Distinguish proposed Nova Engine's apex-shot-then-regress loop. Overlord has periodic chain arcs, no summon/reroll economy.
* Cryo Reactor: make it a freeze-to-shatter COMBO with one dedicated vulnerability window; cap boss slow 20% and avoid overlapping Blizzard's passive slow, Chiller's cooldown, Frost's early ice.
* Holy Halo: likely duplicates Cleanser/Shield Gen/Purifier. Give it an ally-wide emergency revival-of-aura (NOT unit resurrection): once per wave, if 2+ friendly turrets are simultaneously disabled, shorten debuff by 50% for 3 s, cooldown wave-gated. Then Seraph Protocol is localized reactive single-unit support.
* RED thematic separation: Black Hole=space control; Overlord=charge DPS; Cryo Reactor=freeze burst; Holy Halo=emergency defense; new Time Anchor=restore forced transform; Nova Engine=discharge siege; Fate Engine=quality reroll; Rift Harvester=kill economy; Seraph Protocol=localized defensive release. If these overlap in your actual implemented kits, cut the new unit rather than piling on synonyms.

RED DECK CAP / ACCESS RULE
RECOMMENDED: 1 RED (mythic) turret in a standard 5-turret deck, maximum. Gold/purple unrestricted except normal deck uniqueness rule. One mythic per deck protects archetype choice, readable counterplay, F2P fairness, and five-slot deck variety. Allow 2 RED only in a separate explicitly balanced high-chaos event mode; do not make 2 RED a premium privilege. For 1-RED constraint, red heads should be visible and emotionally special; their strength is a signature mechanic, not automatically more raw DPS. A red deck should have meaningful weakness (no guaranteed economy AND control AND hard carry). In co-op, each player may bring 1 red, so total 2 on both boards. If data shows red picks have >55% adjusted win rate or 80%+ inclusion in eligible decks, nerf/counteract, don't simply allow additional red slots.

SIMULATION & ACCEPTANCE TESTS
- Run 10,000 seeded PvP mirrors per deck candidate versus 3 baseline archetypes (fast economy, 1-carry plus 2 support, control) plus 1 co-op survival path. Match skill by equal card levels and equal seed distribution; report adjusted win rate, wave clear, boss TTK, mana at 2/4/6 min, 7-star frequency, units on board, ability uptime.
- Hard fail: chained proc recursion; more than 1 secondary economy action per primary merge; 8th star; supports outside declared topology; non-deterministic multiplayer state; red carry invalidates counter archetypes; visual unreadability at 48 px.
- Initial win-rate gate target 48–52% adjusted for equally skilled pilot decks; up to 55% acceptable only in a narrow counter matchup offset by clear weaknesses. Require a visible 650 ms enemy-board telegraph for disruptive PvP effects.
- Collect pick rate, win rate conditional on opponents, average proc frequency, ability damage contribution, cross-player grief incidents, rage quits after loss of stars, and performance at 5x3 full-board density.
- Visual test at 48 px and 64x55 board cells: neutral metal 75–85% of head; one accent; 1 unmistakeable silhouette landmark; head pivot exactly center; no head-base fused artwork. Idle effects below 10% cell coverage. Growth animations 420–650 ms, no shaking adjacent pieces.
