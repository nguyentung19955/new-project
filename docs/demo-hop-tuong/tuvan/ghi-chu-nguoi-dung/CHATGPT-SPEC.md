# TURRET MERGE — A/B/C IMPLEMENTATION DESIGN SPEC

**Status:** design proposal for owner approval; based on the supplied 57-line brief. **Language:** English. **Baseline:** card LV9. **Version:** 2026-11-07.

## Interpretation legend

- **[OWNER]** = requirement stated in the brief. **[PROPOSED]** = concrete implementation recommendation, not an existing game fact. **[DECIDE]** = owner sign-off needed. All unspecified damage coefficients and durations below are proposed, not extracted from the source.
- D = that turret's *actual* LV9 pre-power-up base attack damage (not a universal shared stat). R = its actual LV9 attacks/second. If the art/game database lacks a value, begin balancing with D=100 and R=1.0; apply the Gold x2.6 or Red x2.7 rarity multiplier **only once** in the damage pipeline. A coefficient such as 3D means 3× that turret's base hit damage. s = current star 1–7. L = permanent card level, and n = max(0,L−9) for levels 9–17.
- Percent increases such as +40% damage are **additive within the same named stat bucket** unless explicitly written ×. Fire rate (attacks/s), **not shot interval**, receives bonuses; +100% fire rate doubles shot count. Crit chance is clamped to 100%; crit damage has no cap. A +30% crit damage bonus means +0.30× to the critical hit multiplier, not +30 percentage points of crit chance.
- Server/lockstep state uses integer ticks at 60 Hz, integer mana and counters, and damage in integer milli-units (e.g., D=100000). Tick durations round to nearest integer once, on cast/spawn. RNG streams are derived from match seed + team ID + stream type; each event is atomic and sorted by (tick, eventPriority, actorCellId, eventSequence).

**Application:** attackDamage = D × rarity × (1 + 0.05n + powerUpDamage + personalDamage + additiveBuffDamage) × product(explicit unique multipliers); fireRate = R × (1 + 0.02n + personalRate + supportRate + tileRate). If existing LV9 D already includes rarity, do **not** multiply rarity again; choose one canonical database convention before coding. The 100/200/400/700 mana power-ups apply +25% of base damage at each completed stage (total +25/50/75/100%); Hydra is exempt. Rarity tier does not affect proc RNG, star count, or mana gains. Special and control effects use the relevant table column only where the skill has numeric strength; e.g. support +20% applied to a +25% buff becomes +30%, *not* +45%. Never multiply the number of targets or generation rate unintentionally.

**Base merge:** both same type + same star, consumed at destination, then one RANDOM deck turret of +1 star, unless its entry specifies a special merge. Max-star units cannot normal-merge. 1 real merge = one Archive event, not two; non-merge transformations, summoning, Copycat, Genesis, Blood marking, Forge buff application, active skills and star-raises are **zero** Archive events. On transformation into a different turret type, the old type's personal stacks disappear. In-game buffs retained on merges are an explicit Forge exception only. Events on a source removed by a merge are resolved before choosing post-merge recipients. Boss and PvP behavior appears in B.

**Level scaling exceptions:** the level table always applies where relevant; explicit LV-cap or probability changes listed in individual turret entries are *additional*, not replacements. Percent modifiers from different level columns do not cross-apply: e.g. a Prism (Attack) gets attack stats, not also +3% utility to mana drop unless specified.

**Star badge geometry on a 40 px turret base:** reserve a 36×11 px zone at the bottom, with 6 px star sprites and 1 px horizontal gap; avoid six tiny overlapping stars. Layouts: ★ centered for 1; ★★ centered for 2; ★★★ centered for 3; ★★★★ in one row (4×6+3×1=27 px); ★★★ / ★★ in two rows for 5; ★★★ / ★★★ for 6. For two-row 5–6, shift the small turret art up 2 px and use a two-row badge 36×16 px; place stars at (12,2)(19,2)(26,2) and centered lower pair at (15.5,9)(22.5,9), or three lower at (12,9)(19,9)(26,9). At 7 use **one 12×12 px star** with a 1 px border and contrasting center, not seven marks. Provide separate 1× and 2× exported PNG; use white-gold filled stars plus a dark outline, with shapes recognizable without color. Star label is not the turret's charge number; separate secondary counters at top-right.

**Breakthrough convention:** earned personal charge milestones use cumulative I +15% fire rate, II +30% crit damage, III +25% damage, IV +10 percentage points crit chance +50% crit damage; IV ring is rainbow. Exceptional units may set their own values. UI ring states none/orange/yellow/blue/purple/rainbow, but use four **milestone** states plus optional rainbow max glow; no full-board flashes. Counters remain uncapped unless a specific turret has a cap. Proc roll per eligible hit is counter-based deterministic, with event ordering stable by cell.

### Event invariants that avoid bugs

1. At a tick, apply boss disables and star changes, then validate command, resolve real merge and unit replacement, then Archive routing, then attacks/status damage, then timer completions and rendering events (consistent order across clients). If an action and timer completion collide, validation uses state at its event position.
2. A unit is identified by entityId, separate from cellId; a consumed entity cannot receive a fly-in stack or complete a pending action. For static aura effects, recompute after every placement/status change.
3. Frozen means **cannot attack, cast, merge or be dragged as actor** for the freeze duration. Frozen may still be a target of external skills unless excluded below. Stone means no attack, skill, buff, or special use; only same-star stone+stone merge is legal. A petrified unit keeps its sealedOriginal but loses active auras until restored.
4. Owner-specified personal points and stacks cannot be shared between copies: new entity starts from its listed initial value. Co-op is 2 separate boards unless stated otherwise; no hidden cross-board aura/stack sharing.
5. Use **one** authoritative random draw for each eligible RNG decision. Input swipe uploads an ordered list of cell IDs; validation uses logical cells, never finger pixel location, network latency, or render frame rate.

## A1. Sixteen substantially reworked units

### 1. COMPASS CANNON — Gold / Attack

**[PROPOSED]** Special merge is the one exception that makes the mechanic achievable: Compass+Compass of equal star **always yields Compass at s+1**, not a random deck card. Each Compass spawns with heading N/E/S/W chosen by seeded RNG and bearing=1. Normal fire: 1.0D, R=0.8/s. Board U-path is split by enemy **route progress**, not physical screen direction: N=0–25%, E=25–50%, S=50–75%, W=75–100% (the labels are a compass-themed tactical map, so the UI legend is essential). Any heading can attack any segment; it **prefers** its headed segment and gains +30% damage +25% crit damage there. Tap the heading badge to rotate one 90° step, cost **15 mana**, maximum once per unit per **20 s**; cannot rotate while frozen/stone. Merge result inherits **destination heading** to give the player control. Same headings: bearing=min(40,a.bearing+b.bearing+1); perpendicular: bearing=max(a.bearing,b.bearing)+1; opposite: bearing=max(1,abs(a.bearing−b.bearing)) plus a one-time **2.0D shockwave** to the nearest 5 normal enemies (elites 50%, bosses 25%). All cases give +12% damage per bearing. Personal breakthrough bearing 4/8/16/32; use generic I–IV. Team jackpot TRUE NORTH: **three same-heading Compass merges** within any rolling **45 s**; all live Compasses gain **+110% damage for 15 s**, refresh rather than stack. Count only live real Compass merges; no Mimic or forced different-type merge. LV10–17: global Attack table; rotate price and jackpot unchanged.
**Risk/readability:** guarantee-Compass +1 is a major exception; cap bearing at 40 and allow user to tap preview before rotating. Show one large arrow + two-digit number above; label path segments only while selected. No free heading rerolls on movement. **[DECIDE]** approve 100% Compass result vs 75% result; whether 15-mana rotation is affordable with growing summon cost. **Assets:** 4 headed turret orientations (64×64 transparent), 4 directional badges (24×24), 2-digit bearing pill (32×14), 4 U-path zone highlights, 12-frame shockwave 128×128, True North 64×64 ring.

### 5. SCRAP FORGE → TWIN ANVIL — Gold / Special

**[OWNER]** Two seeded spawn forms: **Damage Anvil** and **Speed Anvil** (50/50); only identical forms merge, with **any star pair**. **[PROPOSED]** Each Forge entity has persistent stacks, initially s when spawned; maximum 14. ### 6. PRISM THIEF — Gold / Attack

On smartphone, four colour icons must also have distinct shapes: skull/coin/snowflake/diamond. **[DECIDE]** whether true T-shaped adjacency should work even without drawable 4-cell Hamiltonian path (recommended allow tap-all-4 selection, then choose finisher by final tap). **Assets:** four 64×64 colour bodies, 4 colour icons 24×24, 4 stack badges 28×14, 4 small proc VFX 48×48, path/link tile overlay 64×64, four 96×96 finisher bursts, team cooldown icon 24×24.

# B. INTERACTION SPECIFICATION

## B0. Terminology, precedence, anti-exploit conditions

- **Real merge** = exactly two occupied entities consumed/combined by normal pair merge, Compass pair merge, Relay pair merge, Anvil pair merge (including failed roll), or forced boss pair merge. One real merge generates **one** Archive charge routed to one surviving Archive (if one exists) and advances any relevant merge-count jackpot. No effect elsewhere unless explicitly specified.
- **Transformation** = Chrysalis hatch, Copycat mimic, Scramble, or another type replacement; not a merge. **Star adjustment** = Genesis/Merge King/SNIPE/Leech/BURN; no Archive event. **Activation** = Hydra burst, Prism path, Black Hole active, Blood mark/Rite, Genesis summons, etc.; not a merge.
- **Source ownership:** projectile, burn, drone, and singularity damage keeps source entityId even if source later disappears. Personal rewards require the source entity **still exists** and is the eligible event owner; no inheriting kills by successor after a merge. Wyrm burn kill still pays mana to team, but not stacks to an already-dead Wyrm.
- **Support rule:** six Gold Supports affect only orthogonal neighbours with live non-stone entities. A buff from a frozen Support is suppressed while it is frozen; a frozen recipient has its bonuses computed but cannot act. Buffs are calculated at moment of attack, never snapshotted permanently. Max two instances of the same Support type contribute to one recipient; other types add within stat buckets. Buff-effect stats (Forge/Foundry/Blood Rite) are **not** re-amplified by Sanctum or another Support to avoid recursively growing bonuses.
- **Mimic is not defined in the uploaded brief.** The interaction tables assume Mimic = same-star wildcard for **ordinary** merges only; do not silently implement a different behavior if live game code has another one. **Copycat** is the actual Gold unit explicitly defined and can copy stone/frozen with status as stated.
- **Counter reset vs transfer:** archive charge, bearing, Prism colour stacks, Werewolf awakened form, Hive orbit meter, Nemesis judgements, Blood stores, Genesis charges and Anvil stacks are instance-owned. Normal merges reset all except **max non-duplicated Forge buffs** transferred to child. Transforms reset all old-type counters; Copycat establishes brand-new state using its explicit starting rule.
