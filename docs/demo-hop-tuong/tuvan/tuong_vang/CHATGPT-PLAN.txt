3. SIX NEW GOLD TURRET SKILL PLANS — ORIGINAL BALANCE PROPOSALS
============================================================
These numbers are TURRET MERGE design proposals, NOT historical DCDW stats. Assume stars s in 1..7; card LV9 baseline; LV15 max; 4 mana power-ups multiply ordinary base damage but NOT specified special percentages unless called out. Stats below: 1★ / 4★ / 7★ at LV9. On-board bonuses are recalculated after merges and star changes. Each same-unit effect cannot multiply itself recursively.

[P1] EMBER RELAY (Kalaratri inspiration) — cross-shaped fire network / Attack
Skill: each Ember Relay gains Flame Charges via MERGING an Ember Relay; give +1 charge to each same-type Relay in the four CARDINAL neighboring cells (max 5 charges/unit). Each charge gives +12% damage and +3% crit; charged turrets also gain +15% fire rate for 5s upon distribution. At 1★/4★/7★ base attack per shot 100/400/700 (normalized tuning units); fully charged damage bonus +60%, crit +15% at all stars; star increases attack count, not charge multiplier. LV9 damage-per-charge +12%, +1 percentage point/level -> LV15 +18%; crit remains +3%/charge. Synergy: Merge King preserves position; Twin Maker fills network; Railgun row formation. Counters: randomization breaks cross, enemy petrify/star-down; maximum buff 90% at LV15. PVP: only your Relay network; CO-OP: partner attacks not buffed, own Relay only. Art: round gold-rimmed bronze heat sink, four outward cruciform copper flare ports, central white-hot orange ember, NO flamethrower barrel, magenta background.

[P2] QUANTUM CHOIR (Cosmem inspiration) — same-star harmonic self-scaling / Attack
Skill: each Choir counts OTHER friendly board turrets with the same STAR (any type, max 7). Gains +18% damage per match at LV9. At 1★/4★/7★ with 3 matches, bonus +54% each; shot damage scales baseline 90/360/630. At 7★, also +12% attack speed (unlock only); max 7 matches = +126% dmg LV9. Card LV9 18% per match, +2 pp per level -> LV15 30% (cap +210%). Synergy: Twin Maker/Star Cannon/Sync Cannon, careful matching. Counters: Guiltyne-style star-down, chaotic Evolver, overmerge. PVP: own 15 cells only; CO-OP: partner stars don't count to avoid huge scaling. Art: compact circular gold resonator with concentric white-violet sound rings and seven small equidistant prongs, NO cyan network tiles.

[P3] HEX SABOTEUR (Guiltyne inspiration) — adversarial merge disruptor / Control
Skill: merge two Saboteurs: target random enemy turret (server-seeded) and after 1.5s try -1★; cannot reduce under 1★; instead a 1★ target is disabled 2.5s (does NOT delete unit). Success 1★/4★/7★ caster: 35%/50%/65%. Each activation has 8s team cooldown, extra activations queue/drop deterministically; immunity/reflect checked at impact. Card LV9 base success 35% at 1★, +2pp/level -> LV15 47%, +5pp per caster star (max 77% at 7★), hard cap 80%. Synergies: forces opponent to rebuild line bingo; Mirror Gate counters it, Copycat decoys, Merge King restores stars. PVP: opponent debuff; CO-OP: instead random ally turret +1★ with same probability (7★ ineligible), cooldown shared, never grief. Art: round gold mechanical scarab shell with a pointed red tuning-fork jammer and tiny broken-star glyph shape (NOT text), no mirror or padlock.

[P4] FUSION ARCHIVE (Aurora inspiration) — grow through EVERY merge / Attack
Skill: EVERY successful friendly merge adds +1 permanent archive charge to all on-board Fusion Archives; when an Archive is first summoned it inherits 50% of match-global charges (round down). Each charge +2.5% attack damage at LV9, max 40 charges; Archive-on-Archive merges cause short 3x area pulse, 160% attack damage. At 1★/4★/7★ (20 charges) = +50% damage; raw shot baseline 90/360/630. Card LV9 +2.5% per charge, +0.25pp/LV -> LV15 +4% (max +160%); pulse remains 160%. Synergy: Twin Maker/Evolver frequent merges, Scrap Forge growth. Counters: early rush, high-star jam, loss of board space; permanent record is match-local only. PVP/CO-OP: only own merges counted, no transfer; partner may benefit from combat result, not charge propagation. Art: low round gold vault with a fan of five physical punched-metal tally tabs and rising white-blue filing sparks, NOT Pandora chest/keyhole, no tubes.

[P5] HERO FOUNDRY (Kintia inspiration) — create temporary high-speed tiles / Support
Skill: merge Foundry into Foundry: convert random OWN tile (prefer occupied) into a gold HERO tile for 12s. Unit standing there gains attack speed 20%/32%/44% for merged Foundry caster at 1★/4★/7★; no stacking, refresh only; tile persists after unit merge. LV9 base 20% +2pp/level -> LV15 32%; star bonus +4pp/star -> 7★ 56%. Limit two active HERO tiles per player, oldest replaced; no self-target bias. Synergy: Hydra, Star Cannon, Railgun, slower attackers. Counters: forced relocation, board-star disruption; does not buff skill procs or commander effects. PVP only own side; CO-OP each player independent, no cross-board tile effects. Art: gold round industrial stamping press with a raised five-point embossed star platen and triangular black/yellow side braces, NO treasure chest, no hologram mirror.

[P6] BLOOD CIRCUIT (Strigoi inspiration) — universal sacrificial merge / Special
Skill: may be dragged onto any own turret irrespective of STAR/TYPE; replaces the two with one random deck turret at star = min(7, max(stars involved)) (NEVER freely produces a higher star). If consumed target is same star, add 1 Vamp Charge (+6% damage to all Blood Circuits, max 10 charges); if target differs, consume 2 charges and generate a small 220% attack-damage pulse (no free charge generation). 1★/4★/7★ Blood Circuit: basic shot 75/300/525; with 5 charges +30% damage. Card LV9 charge bonus +6%, +0.5pp per level -> LV15 +9% (max +90%). Synergy: resolves 7★ deadlocks, Copycat and Evolver; counter: destroys a valuable tower, loses charges if mismatched, Mirror Gate does NOT block own voluntary merge. PVP/CO-OP identical self-board behavior; no interaction with ally cards. Art: compact round black-red siphon reactor with TWO unequal copper-gold rings and a single crimson fluid ampoule, NO glass three-liquid blender (Fusion Mixer), NO coin or VFX.

BALANCE SAFETY: PVP/CO-OP must resolve on authoritative server, seed all random effects deterministically, apply star cap 7 everywhere, make immunity decisions before star edits, log merge actor/star before mutation; never alter ally board in PVP. Attack speed bonuses require hard floor on firing interval. Check on 15-tile board with simulated 4 mana power-ups before production.
